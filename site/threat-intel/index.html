Found in Criminal enforcement section, tactics bullet list, page 5. PROPOSED LINK  BA005  Rapid Movement  [correspondent_banking]
    Rapid movement is the velocity / layering signal at the heart of correspondent-banking
    monitoring: funds land on an account and clear back out again almost immediately, resting
    only long enough to register a balance change before they are gone. It is not defined by who
    sent the money or where it ends up — those can look entirely ordinary on any single leg — it
    is defined by HOW FAST the funds move and HOW MUCH volume passes through an account relative
    to what it normally holds.
